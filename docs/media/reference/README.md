# Reference frames

## `2026-09-10-lighting-reference.jpg`

**Director-supplied, 2026-09-10.** A generated mockup of THIS build's own frame (the objective banner
reads "Automate the coal feed"; the depth chip reads "+2 m OPEN SKY"; the HOPPER ring is ours), re-lit
and re-textured. The director's words: *"i was playing around with nanobanana to get design inspiration
for UI and lighting, and got to this, and honestly, i love it."*

**This one IS a target, and that is a deliberate exception.** `[[reference-images-mood-not-target]]` and
`docs/TASTE_QUEUE.md` both say a reference is mood and never a thing to match, because overfitting to
someone else's art direction is how a build loses its own. That rule is suspended for this image on the
director's explicit instruction: *"you must cycle back and forth with side by side comparisons of the
reference image lighting and our game until they match perfectly."* It is our frame, so matching it is
not borrowing an identity; it is being shown our own at its best.

**Match the LIGHTING and the ROCK.** What is being chased, in order of how much of the gap each carries:
warm emitters throwing real pools onto rock, occlusion at every cut face, depth haze, light shafts down
open holes, and stone with cobbles and strata instead of 4 px noise. `docs/NORTH_STAR.md` §3 is the
standard; `docs/WORKING.md`'s overnight queue phases 2 and 3 are the work.

**What is NOT being chased:** the mockup's HUD is our HUD, unchanged, seven panels and all. It is not
evidence that the furniture is fine. `docs/NORTH_STAR.md` §4 still holds.

The image also shows a second occupied depth plane. That is a live and unresolved design fork (the
z-axis question, 2026-09-10) and is NOT part of the match: the match is lighting and rock only.
