**Burst 27-29**: Waited while forge worked (0.7s per cycle, 2 ore -> 1 ingot)
  - Forge panel showed: "factory makes 5.2 ingot/min, holds 2"
  - Ingots accumulated in the forge
**Burst 30**: Moved right to collect ingots - SUCCESS!

**Result**: ✓ FORGED 2 INGOTS - OBJECTIVE 2 COMPLETE
- Inventory now shows: 2 ingots
- +2 indicator appeared when ingots were collected

**Key Learnings**: 
- Q key = feed ore to forge (hold to keep feeding)
- Forge takes ~0.7s per cycle, needs 2 ore per ingot
- Ingots are produced and held in forge, then automatically transfer to inventory when you stand near the output
