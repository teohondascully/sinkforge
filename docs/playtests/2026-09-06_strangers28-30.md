   foreground, 11.98 s and two hangs (no response in 120 s) at background under the same load.
2. Turning the render loop off for the burst hung a seat outright; a 60-step physics catch-up was tried
   and dropped with it. Neither addressed the priority.
3. The capture comes after the release, so a refusal is gone from the observation by the frame; the burst
   receipt now carries the last refusal, the cells at its first refusal, every cell broken, and the HUD
