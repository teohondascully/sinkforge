## What the frames show

- **The cap held.** S11 (31 s, D for 100 ticks) and S12 (33 s, D for 240 ticks) both crossed +14 m and
  went on to the world's east edge 32 m from spawn. Eight of nine fell in before the cap; none of three
  after. The east edge itself has no wall: the camera clamps at the world's end and the miner stands at
  the screen's right edge with the ground running under the frame (S11 frame 28, S12 frame 21). Taste
  queue T036.
- **Two of three never mined an ore, and the ring is why.** S11's first move was D for 40 ticks; S12's D
  for 60. Both landed at +7..+10 m, on the drill shaft's lip, and the ring moved from the vein at the pad
  to the shaft's buried ore two metres under the surface (S11 frame 2, S12 frame 3): the nearest ore by
  the ruler, 3.4 m below the body's centre, past the reach from the surface. The how-to still read "at
  your feet". S11 pointed at the ring and got TOO FAR; then pointed at eight more places over 40 s, every
  one refused, and wrote "every interactive object reported out of reach" and "would I keep playing? No."
  S12 pointed once at the true vein 15 m west (TOO FAR, correctly), then walked for 36 s before
  pointing twice more, from the world's east edge at the same vein 37 m off (frame 26: the slash on it, the
  ring on deep ore ten metres away). Eight of nine strangers before this batch pointed at the ring BEFORE walking; the two
  who walked first lost the first rung to it. Fixed as D0436: a hit farther than a reach above or below
  the body ranks behind every hit at the body's own level.
- **S10 spent eighteen seconds felling the air beside the trunk.** Frame 16 (12.6 s): the body beside
  the tutorial tree, the ring tight on the trunk at (680,355), motion streaks on the miner -- the frame
  was taken while the body still slid from the D burst before it. Frame 17: the body most of a metre further on,
  the pointer where the trunk had been, a red slashed square on air. Bursts 17-20, 1,100 ticks of MINE within
  a hand of that spot; the stranger, taught by TOO FAR that the slash means "past your reach", read it as reach and
  moved on ("initial trees didn't cooperate"). Two fixes (D0436): the seat now releases every input and
  runs on until the body is still before it captures (`settled_ticks`, `still` in the observation;
  "settle": false for the raw cut), and a slash held on AIR for a third of a second teaches NOTHING THERE.
- **The tree fell, and its crown stayed up.** S10 frame 33: the felled tree's leaves hang in the air at
  (220-300, 265-320) with no trunk under them. Taste queue T034.
- **Walking is fast against the pad.** D for 40 ticks (0.67 s) carried S11 seven metres, past the forge
  pocket, the vein, the adit and the shaft: the whole authored opening in one short press. RUN_SPEED is
  150 px/s, 9.4 m/s, legacy's constant against a 64 m world. Taste queue T035, not changed here.
- **The whole-stack sentence read.** S10 fed the forge at 4.8 s and waited on it: "1/2" at 6.8, "2/2" at
  8.9, no walking off after the first ingot (S8's failure). One stranger, one reading.
- **The drop-short flash and "POINT" went untested.** No drop landed short and no line was thrown in this
  batch.
- **The seat's stderr is worth reading.** S11's `seat.out` carries 21 `Invariants` reports: the floor
  selection's "second standing surface" heuristic over the adit's stepped cut (authored geometry, a known
  false positive class, D0044), and one "body's own box extends outside the grid" at the east edge,
  reported before the clamp corrected it (D0052 by design). Neither is a defect the strangers met.

## Decided from this batch (D0436)

Three changes, all cheap, all pinned: the ring prefers the body's level (`test_tutorial_teaching`, with a
ruler control that fails under mutation); NOTHING THERE for the air refusal (`test_hints`); the seat
settles before the frame (`test_playtest_input` for the flag; measured live: a 30-tick walk settles in 4
ticks, `still: true`; with "settle": false the same burst captures `still: false`).
