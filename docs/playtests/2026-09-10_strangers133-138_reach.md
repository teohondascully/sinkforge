- **S134 reported "TOO FAR every time" at the CREW RIG "from 7+ different positions"** and called the

  **no `far` refusal in the whole run**. What it did record is `world_edge_right` at burst 30: it had

Neither is dishonest; a stranger writes what it believes. It is why the classification is read from
`observation_*.json` and never from the final message. `[[stranger-protocol-frames-not-report]]`.
