## Not evidence

Three runs, no control for agent skill, and the fix-set they exercised (D0447) was superseded by D0449
before their reports came in. What the batch establishes is the mechanism, per press: the word RINGED
picked a bubble twice and the vein card picked a Drill once. Whether the white reticle and the two new
sentences change the outcome is the next batch's question.
