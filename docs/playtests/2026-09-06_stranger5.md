## Decided from this run (D0428)

1. The rung's target ring stays for the rung's life: full while the how-to is up, at a floor of 0.38
   after it fades, gone only during a finished rung's acknowledgement.
2. The first DROP that lands on the floor docks a lesson: "DROPPED — the stack fell at your feet, and you
   pick up what lies there as you stand. A machine takes a drop only when you stand BESIDE it: a body
   length." `Verbs.last_drop` names where a drop went; the observation carries it one observe wide.
