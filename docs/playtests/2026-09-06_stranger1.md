# Screen-led playtest, stranger 1 -- 2026-09-06

**Who played:** a fresh-context agent (Claude Haiku 4.5) given only the adapter's mechanics -- keys as
physical names, a pointer in 1280x720 pixels, bursts of 1-300 ticks, a screenshot after each -- and told
to play like a curious person without a manual. No source, no coordinates, no coaching. Fable 5.1 (this
session) wrote the mission and read the frames afterwards; it did not steer.

**How:** `playtest/seat.gd` on a fresh tutorial start, 44 bursts, 94 tool calls, 9 minutes of wall time,
about 45 seconds of game time. Journal: `docs/playtests/2026-09-06_stranger1_journal.md`. Frames and the
input log: `tests/body/recordings/playtest_2026-09-06_stranger1/` (local, not tracked).

**Before it could start:** the adapter as handed over could not aim. In a headed window the viewport's
mouse position is the operating system's cursor, so every injected motion left the aim under the tester's
own hand, and with vsync on a window off the active Space stopped answering. Both fixed first (D0419) --
a stranger driven through a broken actuator reports the actuator.

## What the stranger said (verbatim headings from its report)

- **Biggest obstacle:** "trying to mine from the wrong spot felt broken until I discovered you must stand
  exactly on the target circle." Three LMB holds on the right rock from a step too far changed nothing
  on screen.
- **"Two steps to your LEFT"** was read as a walking instruction and taken away from the rock; the player
  went left and uphill, then found the forge by walking RIGHT to the label it could see.
- **The forge's feed:** pressed Q once, unsure it worked, held longer.
- **"Get wood 0/1" never ticked** despite "wood" in the inventory.
- **Told well:** the goal with its count, the on-screen controls, the golden ring on the target, the
  forge's own panel ("2 ore -> 1 ingot, 0.7 s"), the inventory, the region names, contextual lessons.
- **Verdict:** would keep playing; the loop gather -> process -> tool "feels satisfying with clear
  immediate feedback".

## What the frames show (Fable's read of the same screenshots)

- The three fruitless holds were real: the aim square out of reach draws at alpha 0.18, invisible at play

- The "wood" was a sapling: frame 38's second slot is a green block, the counter is right. The player
  could not name the item -- only the selected slot carries a label -- and the wood rung's how-to had faded.
- The lode tooltip ends "57% of the next uni..." at 1280 wide: the key word is the one cut.

- A pale vertical beam beside a natural dip (frame 38): a godray where the surface is lower than its

## Fixed from this run (D0421)

The mine hold names why a held button did nothing (`far` / `sight` / `air`); the observation carries it;
the aim square goes to the refusal red with the bar struck across it while the button is held past the
reach; a TOO FAR lesson docks after a third of a second of it, once. The first three rungs re-worded: the
RING is the pointer ("stand by the RINGED rock at your feet"), no compass words; the forge is "the RINGED
forge"; wood is "a tree's brown TRUNK, not its leaves". The lode tooltip keeps its key word.
