## Decided from this batch (D0438)

The level rule for machines and piles; the near chevron and NEAR_M from the body's centre; "a step to
your LEFT"; the seat waits for the camera; NOTHING THERE at 90 ticks, restarted by a break; and, from
stranger 10's felled tree, the crown crumbles with the trunk (T034 taken provisionally).

## Not done, named

- **Nothing teaches that the way down is to dig** (S15). The ladder's fourth rung says "dig down to the
  crew's drill", but a stranger who wants to descend before then has no sentence for it. A candidate:
  a lesson when the body has walked the pad's width both ways with the first rung done and nothing dug
  ("THE WAY DOWN — the ground is rock you can cut: point at the floor and hold [MINE]"). T037.
- **S13's first instinct, MINE at the ground under the feet, is what S15 needed and S13 did not.** The
  same press is the right move for one mission and the wrong one for the other; the ring and the how-to
  carry the difference, and this batch shows they were not carrying it. Watch the next batch.
- The forge-fed sentence went untested again (no stranger reached a forge with ore in hand).
