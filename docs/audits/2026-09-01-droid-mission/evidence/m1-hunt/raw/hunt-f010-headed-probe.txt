comes from the scene's own get_tree().quit(1 if _failed else 0). A hang
(unguarded runtime error in _ready()) is caught only by the job's
10-min timeout-minutes, not by the wrapper's SCRIPT ERROR detection.

(_failed = true on empty bands), and check_headed_boot.sh has its
own controls (agent-mode discriminator, MIN_COLOURS floor). The probe
is fail-closed on the paths it knows about; the gap is the hang path
only. Not the seventh; a disclosed architectural choice.
