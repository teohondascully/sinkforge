# SINKFORGE — E-menu proposal F: AT THE MACHINE

Generate ONE image, 1920 x 1080, a game interface mockup. Not a collage, not a storyboard.

This is a visual design study for a game menu. The quantities below are a fixture for the drawing.

## The game in four lines

Sinkforge is a side-on pixel-art excavation and factory game. A field engineer digs beneath a permanent
surface rig. Gravity carries goods down the passages the player cuts: the hole is a conveyor. Machines
process and lift material, and the rig issues orders for processed goods.

## The idea this image is testing

This is the state a player will actually be in most often, and no mockup has drawn it yet: **standing
at a machine that has stopped, with the menu open.**

The factory drawing is not here, because the factory is right there in front of them. The pack is not
fully here either. Only the machine, what it wants, and the one stack that answers it.

## Layout

**The world is close and fully rendered**, filling the frame. The engineer stands on the cavity floor
at minus 24 metres, immediately left of the Processor. Rock walls press in. The ore chute's mouth is
above and left, the coal chute's mouth above and right. The Processor is large in frame, dark steel,
and its lamp burns red.

**The panel is small and anchored beside the machine**, floating to the machine's right, not centred on
the screen and not filling it. It is clearly about this object.

Inside the panel, top to bottom:

- The machine's name, **Processor**, and under it its state in its own red: **Out of fuel**.
- A small elevation of the machine itself with its three ports marked: ore intake upper left, coal
  intake upper right, output lower right. Each port is a small mark on the drawing, and the coal intake
  is the lit one.
- What it holds and what it wants, as two short lines: "Holds 6 ore" and "Wants coal".
- One filled brass button: **Feed coal**, with a small figure beside it reading how many it would take.

**Directly beneath the panel, one short row of wells**, five square, small. These are the freight the
engineer is carrying. Four of them are dim. **Coal is lit**, wearing a small red upward triangle in its
top-right corner, the same glyph the machine's lamp is wearing, so the two read as one statement made
twice.

**Beneath that, one more row, dimmer and half the height**: the kit, three wells, collapsed and quiet,
present but out of the way.

**Bottom left, a thin brass load bar** reading 48 of 90, small, ambient, not the subject of this screen.

## The fixture

FREIGHT, 48 of 90 carried: Clay 24 · Coal 12 · Iron-bearing ore 8 · Iron ingot 3 · Glimmer 1
KIT, free: Torch 4 · Rope 2 · Plate Press 1

The Processor is the target. The coal well is the lit one. The Plate Press is selected in the pack but
is not the subject of this screen, so it carries only a quiet brass outline.

## Palette and rendering

Rock at this depth reads cool grey-blue, near black in the dug space, with a warm torch pool on the
cavity floor and the machine's red lamp throwing colour onto the rock beside it.

Interface ground #12141D, wells #1C1F29, edges #333B4D with a #8594AD top bevel so the panel reads as
raised. Brass accent #CCA84D. Text #CCD4E3, dim #8A94A8.

Item inks: clay #AD7A4F · coal #3D404A · iron-bearing ore #6B7A9E · iron ingot #CCD6EB · glimmer
#3DC7D2 · rope #C7A870 · torch #FFC25C.

Machine lamp, out of fuel: #F54233.

The game authors its interface on a 640 x 360 grid and presents it at 2x. Keep the panel small, crisp
and on-grid, with small confident type and tabular figures. The world is the large thing in this image.

Show the screen at rest, settled and sharp.
