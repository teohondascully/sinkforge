# SINKFORGE — E-menu proposal A: THE LOAD

Generate ONE image, 1920 x 1080, a game interface mockup. Not a collage, not a storyboard.

This is a visual design study for a game menu. The quantities below are a fixture for the drawing.

## The game in four lines

Sinkforge is a side-on pixel-art excavation and factory game. A field engineer digs beneath a permanent
surface rig. Gravity carries goods down the passages the player cuts: the hole is a conveyor. Machines
process and lift material, and the rig issues orders for processed goods.

## The idea this image is testing

The pack has a weight limit, and that limit is the whole screen. Ore, rock and refined goods are
freight and cost load. Placeable machines, rope and torches are kit and ride free. So the menu answers
one question in one glance: **what comes out so the ingots fit?**

Make the load a physical object, not a number in a corner.

## Layout

**Far left, full height: the load column.** A tall narrow gauge, filled to 48 of 90. The fill is
stratified: each freight item is its own band, sized by how much of the load it takes, painted in that
item's own ink. Clay is the thick band, then coal, then ore, then a thin ingot band, then a sliver of
glimmer. Empty space above the fill, brass-marked at the top with 90.

**Beside the gauge: the freight list, aligned to its own bands.** A fine leader line runs from each
band to its name and count, so reading down the gauge and reading down the list are the same motion.
Header: FREIGHT · 48 / 90.

**Below a hairline: the kit.** Three rows sitting directly on the surface, no bands, no gauge, lighter
weight. Header: KIT · rides free.

**Lower left: the selected item.** Plate Press, with a small dimensional drawing of the machine, the
line "2 Iron ingots to 1 Plate", and one filled brass button reading **Place in shaft**. The button is
the most actionable thing on the screen.

**Right third: the shaft.** A compact cutaway of the excavation, with the engineer drawn on it. Beneath
it, the order as six pips in a row: two filled solid, three filled hollow, one empty outline, and the
line "Ingots for the winch". Beneath that, small: Glimmer, "Kept it anyway."

## The fixture

FREIGHT, 48 of 90 carried: Clay 24 · Coal 12 · Iron-bearing ore 8 · Iron ingot 3 · Glimmer 1
KIT, free: Torch 4 · Rope 2 · Plate Press 1

Plate Press is selected, washed pale brass with a slim marker.

**The coal band and the coal row both wear a small upward triangle in red.** In this game a starving
machine wears that triangle, and the stack that would feed it wears the same triangle in the same
colour. The Processor at minus 24 metres is out of fuel, and the player is carrying twelve coal.

## The shaft, drawn the same way in every proposal

Surface rig at top right with a Winch Station beside it. Drill at minus 12 metres on the left. An
excavated ore chute descends from the Drill, bending twice, widening into a collection cavity above the
Processor at minus 24 metres. A Coal Hopper at minus 18 metres sits right of the ore chute and sends a
separate coal chute down to meet the Processor's intake at a marked junction. The Processor's output
descends right to a Winch Head at minus 28 metres. A powered return climbs the right-hand side from the
Winch Head to the Winch Station. A dotted route leaves the Winch Station and arrives at the Surface Rig,
labelled "Carry to rig" — the one leg the engineer walks. Depth ruler on the left reading minus 12, 18,
24, 28. The engineer stands at minus 20 metres beside the ore chute. The Processor glows red.

## Palette and rendering

Ground #12141D, panels #101218, slot wells #1C1F29, edges #333B4D with a #8594AD top bevel so panels
read as raised. Brass accent #CCA84D. Text #CCD4E3, dim #8A94A8.

Item inks: clay #AD7A4F · coal #3D404A · iron-bearing ore #6B7A9E · iron ingot #CCD6EB · glimmer
#3DC7D2 · rope #C7A870 · torch #FFC25C.

Machine status red #F54233.

The game authors its interface on a 640 x 360 grid and presents it at 2x. Draw as though every element
sits on that grid: crisp on-pixel edges, hairline rules, small confident type at interface size.
Clean sans for numbers and labels with tabular figures. The dimmed pixel-art shaft stays visible around
the panel.

Show the screen at rest, settled and sharp.
